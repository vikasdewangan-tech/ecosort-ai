Waste Categories

What EcoSort AI Can Classify

♻️

Recyclable / Dry Waste

Plastics, metals, paper, glass, and other dry materials that can typically be recycled.

🍃

Organic / Wet Waste

Food scraps, peels, and biodegradable material suited for composting.

🔋

E-Waste

Electronics, batteries, and devices requiring authorized disposal facilities.

⚠️

Hazardous Waste

Chemicals, sharp objects, or materials that require extra care in handling.

🗑️

General Waste

Non-recyclable, non-hazardous items that go to standard waste collection.

AI Limitations

  • Similar-looking materials may be difficult to distinguish.
  • Poor image quality can reduce accuracy.
  • Multiple objects can create ambiguity.
  • Local waste rules vary.
  • AI predictions may be incorrect.
  • Hazardous materials require additional care.
